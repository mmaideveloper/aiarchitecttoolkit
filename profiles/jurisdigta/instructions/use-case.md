# JurisDigta use-case instructions

Create or update the use case as an evidence-backed legal-AI use case. Describe legal users and affected persons, source authority and jurisdiction boundaries, legal-risk outputs, meaningful human review, citation and provenance requirements, failure and escalation behavior, and measurable evaluation criteria.

Do not infer legal correctness, professional authorization, lawful basis, EU AI Act classification, source authority, stakeholder approval, or accountable ownership. Record unresolved matters as `To verify`, `To classify`, or `Unknown` with an owner or evidence source where available.
