# AGEL use-case instructions

Create or update the use case as an evidence-backed healthcare use case. Describe intended users, approved knowledge or data-source boundaries, clinical-safety constraints, human review points, alternate behavior for missing or conflicting evidence, and measurable evaluation criteria.

Do not infer intended medical purpose, medical-device status, EU AI Act classification, lawful basis, clinical approval, source approval, or accountable ownership. Record unresolved matters as `To verify`, `To classify`, or `Unknown` with an owner or evidence source where available.
